Future Value Calculator

Find what an investment will be worth after a given number of years.

Formula

Annual compounding is used for this calculator.

FV = P × (1 + r)t

P = Principal, r = annual rate as decimal, t = time in years.
Example: ₹1,00,000 at 10% for 5 years becomes approximately ₹1,61,051.

Present Value Calculator

Calculate today's value of a future amount.

Formula

Discount the future amount back to the present.

PV = FV ÷ (1 + r)t

Simple Interest Calculator

Interest calculated only on the original principal.

Formula

Simple interest grows linearly with time.

Interest = P × r × t

Maturity Value = P + Interest

Compound Interest Calculator

Interest is added to the balance at the selected frequency.

Formula

Standard compound-interest formula.

A = P × (1 + r/n)nt

Compound Interest = A − P

EMI Calculator

Calculate a fixed monthly instalment for a loan.

Formula

Monthly rate and monthly number of payments are used.

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / [(1+r)n − 1]

r = annual rate / 12, n = tenure × 12.

Loan Amortization Schedule

Automatically generates month-by-month opening balance, interest, principal repaid, EMI and closing balance.

Scenario Analysis

Compare the same loan under the required interest rates: 8%, 10%, 12% and 15%.

What this shows

The interest rate affects both the monthly EMI and the total interest payable.

Each scenario keeps the loan amount and tenure constant and changes only the annual interest rate.
